# Breathing Patterns settings

Four-phase breathing configuration.

## Information kept

- **Preset** — Selected shipped breathing-pattern preset or custom phase choice.
- **Phase durations** — Configured durations for inhale, hold, exhale and hold.
- **Session duration** — Configured total breathing-pattern duration.
