# Audio settings

Saved audio preferences.

## Information kept

- **Cue volume** — Saved volume used for practice cues.
- **Music volume** — Saved volume used for practice music.
- **Cue choices** — Selected cue sounds for the exposed practice events.
- **Practice music** — Selected music or no music for each practice family.
